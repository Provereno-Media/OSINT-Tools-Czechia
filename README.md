# OSINT-Tools-Czechia
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cb/Flag_of_the_Czech_Republic.svg?utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original" alt="Czechia_FLAG"/>

A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Czechia. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Czech; several also offer English interfaces. Since 17 December 2025 the Register of Beneficial Owners (Evidence skutečných majitelů) is no longer open to the general public, following the CJEU judgment of 22 November 2022 (C-37/20, C-601/20) and rulings of Czech supreme courts: access is limited to public authorities, AML-obliged persons and persons who prove a legitimate interest through a court. Vehicle owner data and the small-vessel register are not public. Many justice.cz applications block automated access, and several state portals work only with JavaScript enabled. Police search databases and the CZ.NIC WHOIS log every query.

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [Národní katalog otevřených dat (National Open Data Catalogue)](https://data.gov.cz/) — national catalogue of open datasets, applications and data providers, run by the Digital and Information Agency; browse by dataset, provider or keyword. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Český statistický úřad (Czech Statistical Office)](https://csu.gov.cz/) — official statistics on population, economy, regions and labour market, with links to the public database, DataStat and the statistical geoportal. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [Volby.gov.cz (Election Results)](https://volby.gov.cz/) — official results of presidential, parliamentary, Senate, regional, municipal and European elections and referendums, back to 1990. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [e-Sbírka (Electronic Collection of Laws and International Treaties)](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/) — official gazette of laws, decrees, government regulations and international treaties; free public access. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Databáze soudních rozhodnutí (Court Decisions Database)](https://rozhodnuti.justice.cz/) — Ministry of Justice database of anonymised court decisions; search by case number, court and full text. *Lang: CS.* 
- [InfoSoud (Court Proceedings Tracker)](https://infosoud.gov.cz/InfoSoud) — status and timeline of court proceedings; search by court and case number (spisová značka). *Lang: CS.* 
- [NALUS (Constitutional Court Decisions)](https://nalus.usoud.cz/) — Constitutional Court judgments and resolutions; full-text search, ECLI, case number, dates, judge rapporteur, challenged act. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Burza cenných papírů Praha (Prague Stock Exchange)](https://www.pse.cz/) — listed shares, bonds, funds and indices, issuer pages with prospectuses, official price list and trading statistics. *Lang: CS, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [ARES – Administrativní registr ekonomických subjektů (Administrative Register of Economic Entities)](https://ares.gov.cz/) — Ministry of Finance search aggregating data on economic entities from source state registers; search by IČO (company ID) or name; data informational only. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Veřejný rejstřík a Sbírka listin (Public Register and Collection of Documents)](https://or.justice.cz/ias/ui/rejstrik) — commercial and other public registers: legal form, address, statutory bodies, shareholders, capital, liquidation, plus filed documents (articles, annual reports, financial statements); search by name, IČO, file number or related person; free. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Insolvenční rejstřík – ISIR (Insolvency Register)](https://isir.justice.cz/isir/common/index.do) — insolvency proceedings since 2008 with full case documents; search by name, IČO, birth date, municipality, court or case number. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Živnostenský rejstřík – RŽP (Trade Licensing Register)](https://rzp.gov.cz/verejne-udaje/cs/udaje/vyber-subjektu) — current and historical trade licences of sole traders and companies; search by IČO, name or address. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Registr plátců DPH (VAT Payers Register)](https://adisspr.mfcr.cz/cgi-bin/adis/idph/int_dp_prij.cgi?ZPRAC=FDPHI1&poc_dic=2) — VAT registration status, tax office, published bank accounts and "unreliable payer" flag; search by DIČ (tax ID). *Lang: CS.* 
- [Otevřená data veřejného rejstříku (Public Register Open Data)](https://dataor.justice.cz/) — bulk open-data dumps of the public registers; current year updated daily, past years annually. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Úřad průmyslového vlastnictví – Databáze ochranných známek (Industrial Property Office – Trademark Database)](https://www.upv.gov.cz/informacni-zdroje/narodni-databaze/databaze-ochrannych-znamek) — entry point to searches of Czech, WIPO and EUIPO trademarks valid in Czechia; the office also runs patent, utility-model and design databases; open data available. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Hlídač státu (State Watchdog)](https://www.hlidacstatu.cz/) — NGO platform linking companies to public contracts, subsidies, party sponsorship and politically exposed persons; company and person profiles with ownership/board relationships; search by name or IČO; free (registration only for bookmarks). *Lang: CS.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Nahlížení do katastru nemovitostí (Cadastre of Real Estate Viewer)](https://nahlizenidokn.cuzk.gov.cz/) — selected cadastral data on parcels, buildings, units and ownership sheets (LV), cadastral proceedings status and map view; free, no registration; full extracts are paid. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Geoportál ČÚZK (ČÚZK Geoportal)](https://geoportal.cuzk.gov.cz) — national surveying and cadastre geoportal: Geoprohlížeč web map viewer, datasets (incl. ZABAGED), network services, INSPIRE and open data. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [Archiv ČÚZK (ČÚZK Archive Map Viewer)](https://ags.cuzk.gov.cz/archiv/) — scanned archival maps (incl. Stable Cadastre imperial imprints) and archive of aerial survey photographs. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Veřejný dálkový přístup RÚIAN (Register of Territorial Identification, Addresses and Real Estate – Public Access)](https://vdp.cuzk.cz/) — official register of addresses, buildings, parcels and administrative units. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Národní geoportál INSPIRE (National INSPIRE Geoportal)](https://geoportal.gov.cz) — national map client with INSPIRE themes (cadastral parcels, addresses, orthoimagery, land use, geology), environmental registers (IPPC, pollutant release), and a historical 1950s orthophoto layer. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [Mapy.com (formerly Mapy.cz)](https://mapy.com/) — Seznam.cz map portal with proprietary Czech data: basic, outdoor, winter, traffic, aerial and 3D views, POI search. *Lang: CS, EN.* 💲 Freemium: main maps and search free; premium tier since Dec 2024 for extra features

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Kontrola tachometru (Odometer Check)](https://www.kontrolatachometru.cz/) — Ministry of Transport lookup of odometer readings recorded at technical inspections; input: full 17-character VIN + captcha. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Pátrání po vozidlech (Police Search for Stolen Vehicles)](https://policie.gov.cz/patrani-vozidla) — Police of the Czech Republic register of vehicles and plates reported stolen; input: full plate number or VIN; queries are logged. *Lang: CS.* 
- [eDálnice – Ověření platnosti (Electronic Vignette Validity Check)](https://edalnice.gov.cz/cs#validity-section) — official motorway vignette e-shop; checks whether a vehicle has a valid e-vignette or exemption; input: country of registration + plate. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Česká kancelář pojistitelů – Informační středisko (Czech Insurers' Bureau – Information Centre)](https://ic.ckp.cz/ICwww/servlet?_page=searchSPZ&lngID=1) — finds the liability insurer of a Czech-registered vehicle; input: plate (SPZ). *Lang: CS.*

### Maritime
- [Plavební rejstřík (Inland Navigation Register)](https://plavebni-rejstrik.plavebniurad.cz/index.asp) — State Navigation Administration public register of vessels (other than small craft); search by 6-digit registration number or 8-digit ENI; deleted vessels show last recorded state. Small-vessel register is not public. *Lang: CS.* 
- [RIS LAVDIS (River Information Services)](https://lavdis.cz/) — State Navigation Administration waterway service: water levels, waterway/lock status, notices to skippers, ports, cameras, vessel position tracking/AIS section. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [Geoportál Státní plavební správy (State Navigation Administration Geoportal)](https://geoportal.plavebniurad.cz/) — maps of Czech waterways and navigation structures, Inland ENC charts, historical navigation map sheets. *Lang: CS.* 

### Aviation
- [Letecký rejstřík (Civil Aviation Authority Aircraft Register Search)](https://lr.caa.gov.cz/letecky-rejstrik) — search of the Czech civil aircraft register (OK- registrations); the register records owner, operator, registration mark, type and serial number, liens, registration/deletion dates. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Úřad pro civilní letectví – Letecký rejstřík (Civil Aviation Authority – Aircraft Register)](https://www.caa.gov.cz/rejstriky/letecky-rejstrik/) — register rules, archived register materials and register statistics. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Flightradar24](https://www.flightradar24.com/) — global live flight tracker covering Czech airspace and OK- registered aircraft; flight status and airport data. *Lang: EN.* 💲 Freemium: live map free; advanced features require subscription (7-day trial)

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Centrální registr oznámení (Central Register of Declarations)](https://cro.gov.cz/verejnost/funkcionari) — Ministry of Justice register of public officials' conflict-of-interest declarations; search by official. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Pátrání po osobách (Police Search for Wanted and Missing Persons)](https://policie.gov.cz/patrani-osoby) — wanted and missing persons; search by name, sex, status, region, district; queries are logged. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Poslanecká sněmovna – Poslanci (Chamber of Deputies – Members)](https://www.psp.cz/sqw/hp.sqw?k=192) — current deputies with profiles, by party club, committee and region; links to deputies' declarations register and voting records. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [ÚDHPSH – Výroční finanční zprávy (Party Finance Oversight Office – Annual Financial Reports)](https://udh.gov.cz/vyrocni-financni-zpravy-stran-a-hnuti) — annual financial reports of political parties and movements 2017–2025; the office also publishes campaign accounts and sanctions. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Česká advokátní komora – Vyhledávání advokátů (Czech Bar Association – Lawyer Search)](https://www.cak.cz/vyhledavani-advokatu) — registered lawyers and trainees; search by name, city, firm, registration number, practice area, language. *Lang: CS.*
- [Hlídač státu – Platy politiků (Politicians' Salaries)](https://platy.hlidacstatu.cz/politici) — income from public offices and accumulation of functions for ~2,000 deputies, senators, ministers and regional/city councillors. *Lang: CS.*
- [Hlídač státu – Politický sponzoring (Political Sponsorship)](https://www.hlidacstatu.cz/sponzori) — individual and corporate donors to political parties, compiled from ÚDHPSH party reports; links to person and company profiles. *Lang: CS.*
- [Firmy.cz (Business Directory)](https://www.firmy.cz/) — Seznam.cz directory of 750,000+ businesses and professionals; search by keyword, location, category. *Lang: CS, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [Registr smluv (Register of Contracts)](https://smlouvy.gov.cz/) — public-sector contracts published under Act No. 340/2015 Coll.; search by publisher, subject, contracting party, date, value; open data and API. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Věstník veřejných zakázek (Public Procurement Journal)](https://vvz.nipez.cz/) — official journal of public procurement notices (NIPEZ). *Lang: CS.* 
- [NEN – Národní elektronický nástroj (National Electronic Tool)](https://nen.nipez.cz/) — state e-procurement system for all contract categories; tenders and contracting-authority profiles. *Lang: CS, EN.* 
- [Zakázky GOV (Public Tenders Portal)](https://zakazky.gov.cz/) — aggregates tenders from different procurement systems in one place; full-text and AI-assisted search. *Lang: CS.* 
- [Hlídač smluv (Contracts Watchdog)](https://www.hlidacstatu.cz/Smlouvy) — searchable mirror of the Register of Contracts (~9 million contracts) with risk reports, e.g. contracts with politically linked firms or newly founded firms. *Lang: CS.*
- [Hlídač státu – Veřejné zakázky (Public Tenders Search)](https://www.hlidacstatu.cz/verejnezakazky) — ~1.5 million tenders; search by IČO, supplier, contracting authority, CPV, VVZ number, value, dates. *Lang: CS.*
- [Hlídač dotací (Subsidies Watchdog)](https://www.hlidacstatu.cz/dotace) — state, EU and regional subsidies since 2010 compiled from IsRed, EU funds, SZIF and regional sources; search by IČO, recipient, project, amount. *Lang: CS.*

## WHOIS
- [CZ.NIC WHOIS](https://www.nic.cz/) — .cz registry search by domain name, contact ID or nameserver set; use limited to network administration or domain disputes; queries logged, bulk harvesting blocked. *Lang: CS.*
- [CZ.NIC – Seznam registrátorů (.cz Registrars List)](https://www.nic.cz/whois/registrars/) — 48 accredited .cz registrars with services offered and certification ratings; useful to identify and contact a domain's registrar. *Lang: CS.*
- [CZ.NIC – Statistiky (Registry Statistics)](https://stats.nic.cz/) — .cz domain registration and DNSSEC counts since 2008, DNS traffic, annual Domain Report, REST API. *Lang: EN.*
- [ICANN Lookup](https://lookup.icann.org/) — registration data for gTLD domain names and Internet number resources. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records, subdomain discovery and ASN lookup. *Lang: EN.*
